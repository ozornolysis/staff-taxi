About this planner

A Monday–Saturday p.m. · D Monday–Saturday a.m. · G Sunday a.m. · K Sunday p.m.

The Staff Taxi network is a concession to help staff report for duty at the required place and time. This planner uses taxi schedule data from CABS, is offered in beta “as is”, and may not be 100% perfect. It does not book taxis – bookings still need to be made in CABS.

Staff are expected to use night buses and the Night Tube whenever possible. A Staff Oyster Pass is valid on all TfL rail services, TfL buses, and National Rail (standard class) between Amersham–Marylebone, Euston–Watford Junction, Fenchurch Street–Upminster, Finsbury Park–Moorgate (via Drayton Park only), Kentish Town–Elephant & Castle or London Bridge, and Stratford–Liverpool Street.

Journeys are searched within one schedule code only. A ⚠ at a change means the two taxis use different stop points at that place.
